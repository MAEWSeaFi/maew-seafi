MAEW SeaFi · Personal Finance & Career Planner for Seafarers

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

MAEW SeaFi ("the App") is a personal finance and career planning application for seafarers, provided by Maew MK ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the App handles information and the choices available to you.

1. Information you enter

You may enter details such as vessel and contract information, rank, salary components, financial planning allocations, bank-transfer records, onboard balances, deductions, and contract history. These records may include sensitive personal financial information.

2. Where information is stored

The current Android version stores the information you enter locally on your device using the App's embedded web interface and its local storage. The App does not require an account to use its core features. We do not operate a server to receive or store the financial records you enter in this version.

3. Backups and file access

You may choose to export a backup file containing your App data, or import a backup file to restore it. Exported files are saved to a location you select through Android's file interface. Imported files are selected by you. Backup files may contain your contract, salary, and financial records, so please store and share them carefully. If you upload a backup to a cloud service or send it to someone, that service or recipient handles the copy under their own practices.

4. Data sharing and external services

Based on the reviewed Android version 1.3.0 (version code 8), the App does not include an advertising SDK or an analytics SDK, and it does not declare Android internet permission. We do not sell or share the financial information you enter with advertisers. Android system services used to choose, save, or print files may process those actions according to your device settings and the services you choose.

5. Your choices and deletion

You control the information you enter into the App and the backup files you export. You can manage or delete records using the App's available controls. Uninstalling the App generally removes its local app data, subject to Android device behavior. Exported backup files are separate and must be deleted from their saved locations by you. Importing a backup can replace existing App data; review the App's warnings before proceeding.

6. Security

We aim to minimize data exposure by keeping the App's core records on your device. However, no device or storage method is completely risk-free. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep exported backup files secure. We do not claim that exported backup files are encrypted.

7. Children

The App is intended for seafarers and other users managing their own work and finances, and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated server.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App's functionality or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are published.
